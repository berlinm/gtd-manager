How to use this system

Capture everything, clarify it into the right list, and review regularly. Expand a section below for detail.

1

Capture

Dump anything on your mind into the Inbox using the bar at the top. Don't decide yet — just capture.

2

Clarify

Process each inbox item one at a time. Decide what it is and pick a disposition.

3

Organize

Clarified items land in the right list automatically. Navigate them from the sidebar.

4

Review

A short Daily Review to orient; a full Weekly Review to keep the system current.

Item types
Next Action
A single, concrete physical step you can start without more planning — "Email Sarah about the report", not "Work on project".
Project
Any outcome needing more than one action. A project with no next action is flagged as stuck. Name it as an outcome: "Website launched".
Waiting For
Something you're tracking that depends on someone else. Set a follow-up date so you remember to check in.
Agenda Item
Something to raise with a specific person or meeting. Not an action — no dates. Mark it raised once discussed.
Someday / Maybe
Ideas to keep visible without committing. Reviewed weekly; promote to a project when ready.
Reference
Information for later — no action attached. Supports Markdown and file attachments. Searchable.
Meeting & Meeting Note
A meeting is a recurring target; start a session each time it happens to capture notes. Process notes after the meeting like inbox items.

Reviews
Daily Review
Quick morning orientation: what's scheduled today, what's due, follow-ups to nudge, stuck projects. End by picking a short focus list — it clears on the next daily review.
Weekly Review
A thorough audit in nine steps — clear the inbox, review stuck and on-hold projects, check deadlines and waiting-fors, scan someday/maybe and agenda. Do it weekly on a fixed day.
